How it works Strola+ FAQ

Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

Strola is built so that there is almost nothing to have a privacy policy about. The app has no accounts, no login, and no server of ours. Your health data is read on your device and stays there. This page explains exactly what the app touches, what (little) leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

What Strola reads, and where it stays

  • Steps and motion. Strola reads your step count from your iPhone's motion sensors and from Apple Health (which can include your Apple Watch) to measure progress toward the goal you set. These readings are processed on your device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. Strola reads steps only. It does not read any other health data, and it never writes to Apple Health.
  • Your blocked apps. App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time technology (Family Controls). Apple's design gives Strola opaque tokens rather than app names, so we cannot see which apps you chose, and that information never leaves your device.
  • Your history. Your daily records, meaning steps walked, goals met, and streaks, are stored on your device. If a future version offers iCloud backup, that history syncs only to your personal, private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and we cannot read.

The one thing that can leave your phone

With your permission implied by default (and revocable any time), Strola sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service, so we can see things like how many people finish setup or complete their first goal. These signals:

  • contain event names and counts only, for example "onboarding step completed" or "goal celebrated";
  • never contain your step counts, health data, app selections, name, email, precise location, or any advertising identifier;
  • are anonymized by TelemetryDeck so they are not linked to you, and are never used for tracking or advertising.

You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage analytics, and Strola keeps working exactly the same.

Purchases

Strola+ purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. We receive only an anonymous confirmation that a subscription is active so the app can unlock Strola+ features.

Notifications

Reminders and celebrations are local notifications scheduled on your device. Strola does not send push notifications from a server.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Apple's built-in reporting may share a diagnostic with us if you've opted into sharing analytics with developers in iOS Settings, and Strola may record an anonymous "a crash happened" signal (crash type and app version only, never the contents of your data).

What we never do

  • There are no accounts, profiles, or logins.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data. There is nothing to sell.
  • No advertising, ad SDKs, or tracking across apps and websites.
  • Strola does not read any health data beyond your step count.

Deleting your data

Your data lives on your device, so deleting Strola deletes it. Step data recorded in Apple Health belongs to Health, not Strola, and is managed in the Health app. Analytics signals are anonymous, so they can't be connected back to you, by us or by TelemetryDeck.

Children

Strola is a self-management tool for people who choose to block their own apps. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not include parental controls or child accounts.

Changes

If we change this policy, for example when iCloud backup ships, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be called out in the app's release notes.
