How it works

Three choices. One honest deal.

You set the terms once. After that, Strola holds you to them — every morning, until the walk is done.

Step 1

Pick your apps

Choose what traps you — social, video, games. Strola shields them with Apple's Screen Time technology, so the block is real.

Step 2

Set your steps and window

A goal you'll actually do — 500 to 10,000 steps — and the hours it applies. Mornings are where doomscrolling goes to die.

Step 3

Walk to unlock

Your iPhone and Apple Watch count the steps. Hit the goal and your apps open early — with a celebration you'll walk for.

The streak

Miss a day and the flame goes out.

Every completed walk fills a day on your calendar and grows the number on the flame. It follows you everywhere you look — so quitting is never quiet.

  • Widgets put the streak on your home and lock screen.
  • Live Activity counts your walk down in the Dynamic Island.
  • Badges mark the milestones — 7 days, 100k steps, comebacks.
  • Share cards let a good week leave the phone.

Privacy

Your steps never leave your phone.

No account. No login. Strola reads your step count on-device and can't see which apps you block — Apple's Screen Time design keeps that private even from us. The only thing that ever leaves the phone is anonymous feature-usage counts, and you can turn those off.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Can't I just delete the app to get around it?

You could — Strola is a commitment device, not a jail. But the shield, your streak on the line, and the walk being the fastest way back in are usually enough friction to send you outside instead. That's the point: make the good choice the easy one.

Does it count steps from my Apple Watch?

Yes. Strola reads steps from your iPhone's motion sensors and from Apple Health, which includes your Watch — whichever source is higher counts, so a phone-in-pocket walk and a watch-only walk both work.

What happens if I miss a day?

The day shows up honestly on your calendar, the streak resets, and tomorrow is a comeback — there's a badge for that. Your apps unlock when the window ends either way; Strola never locks you out all day.

Which apps can it block?

Any apps, categories, or websites you choose using Apple's Screen Time picker — social media, video, games, all of it. Strola never sees the list; Apple keeps it private by design.

Is my health data safe?

Your step counts are read on-device and never transmitted anywhere. There's no server and no account. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
